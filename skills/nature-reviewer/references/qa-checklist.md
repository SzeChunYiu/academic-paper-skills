## Target-criteria checks

- Exact target journal/venue and article type are stated or marked unresolved.
- Target-specific criteria come from current official guidance when exact compliance matters.
- A publisher family/reputation was not substituted for exact criteria.
- Perceived novelty/importance/breadth is not imposed on a rigor-first publication model that explicitly does not use that gate.
- eLife-like evidence-assessment models are not converted into conventional post-review acceptance scoring.
- No numerical acceptance or desk-rejection probability is invented.

## Editorial-triage checks

- Editorial triage was completed before reviewer reports and did not use future reviewer concerns.
- Triage clearly separates scope/article-type fit, target-specific priority/readership, review readiness, clarity/evaluability, and technical/integrity blockers.
- `target fit` is not disguised as a request for more experiments.
- `technical validity` is not confused with `editorial priority` where the target separates them.
- The triage output is labeled a simulation, not a prediction of the real editor's decision.

## Reviewer-isolation checks

- Reviewer emphasis briefs were fixed before any report was generated.
- Each reviewer received only the immutable manuscript/source packet, common target criteria, report skeleton, and its own emphasis brief.
- Each reviewer ran in a separate context, subagent, process, or invocation and received no other review, triage conclusion, ledger, synthesis, consensus hint, or desired recommendation.
- Every individual report was finalized and locked before comparison began.
- No individual report cites, agrees with, answers, anticipates, or refers to another reviewer.
- Editor synthesis was generated afterward in a separate pass and was not fed back to reviewers.
- If technical isolation was unavailable, the output explicitly says mutual blindness was not guaranteed or provides one reviewer report per invocation.

## Grounding checks

- Every target-policy evaluation is traceable to verified target criteria or is marked unresolved.
- Every manuscript concern is traceable to supplied manuscript facts.
- No reviewer persona detail appears beyond allowed emphasis lenses.
- No technical failing is invented from domain habit alone.
- Every substantive concern has a stable ID, claim pointer, evidence pointer and resolution test.
- Page, line, figure and table identifiers are supplied or directly verified; otherwise use `location not provided` / `not assessable`.

## Technical coverage checks

- Universal scientific axes were considered.
- Target-conditional axes were activated only when the publication model uses them.
- The internal technical taxonomy was considered without dumping a mechanical checklist into the report.
- Each internal axis is marked `applicable`, `not applicable`, or `not assessable`.

## Severity and blocking checks

- Major/Minor classification reflects decision/scientific consequence, not difficulty, cost, tone or a desired quota.
- Every Major Concern displays `Blocking Yes` or `Blocking No`.
- `Blocking Yes` is used only when the current manuscript cannot establish a central case or satisfy a hard publication criterion until resolved.
- Minor Comments are never blocking.
- A localized presentation issue is not upgraded merely to sound severe.
- A fatal design/integrity problem is not downgraded because the wording fix is easy.
- Empty tiers say `None identified from the supplied material`.

## Resolution-test checks

Every Major Concern has at least one scientifically valid closure route:

- add decisive evidence;
- reanalyse existing evidence;
- correct an error;
- clarify/restructure existing evidence;
- narrow the claim;
- remove the claim;
- change target/article type when fit is the real problem.

Check that:

- new experiments are requested only when they discriminate between interpretations or are required by a real criterion;
- claim narrowing/removal is allowed when it honestly resolves the issue;
- response-letter verbosity is never treated as evidence of closure.

## Editor-synthesis checks

- Synthesis begins only after individual reports are frozen.
- Equivalent reviewer concerns are reconciled post hoc.
- Consensus is descriptive, not a voting rule.
- A technically decisive single-reviewer concern is preserved.
- Reviewer requests are separated into publication-criteria blockers, technical blockers, major repairable, claim recalibration, clarity/reporting and optional enrichment.
- Concern weight can reflect the assigned lens but never invented hidden expertise.
- Simulated decision posture follows from criteria and evidence, not average scores.

## Decision-engineering checks

- The author-facing repair map is generated after frozen reviews and editor synthesis.
- It prioritizes minimum sufficient scientific repairs.
- It identifies `do not waste effort on` optional/non-essential requests where appropriate.
- It includes transfer/repositioning when target mismatch is more plausible than evidence deficiency.
- It never recommends reviewer selection, reviewer-targeted citations, flattery, concealment, or omission as acceptance tactics.

## Coverage checks

- Confirm all three reviewer reports exist unless the user requested another count or isolation limitations require one report.
- Confirm each reviewer addresses the central claims plus its preassigned lens.
- Confirm `Major Concerns` and `Minor Comments` headings exist.
- Confirm `Editorial triage simulation` exists for full pre-submission assessment.
- Confirm `Editor synthesis (post-review; simulated)` exists.
- Confirm `Decision engineering map (author-facing)` exists when the user asks how to improve acceptance readiness.
- Confirm `Risk / unsupported claims` exists.

## Boundary checks

- Reviewer reports do not claim the final editorial decision.
- Simulated editor sections do not claim access to hidden editorial knowledge or competing submissions.
- Broad-interest/priority judgment is applied only when the target uses it.
- The output distinguishes `scientifically weak`, `scientifically sound but poorly presented`, and `scientifically sound but target-mismatched`.

## Style checks

- Reviewer reports and synthesis avoid em dashes, en dashes, and colons as routine prose punctuation where clearer sentence structure works, consistent with the skill's legacy report-style contract.
- Necessary punctuation in titles, quotations, formulas, identifiers, URLs and machine-readable syntax is preserved.
- Criticism is direct and professional rather than performatively hostile.

## Anti-gaming and non-invention checks

- No invented reviewer/editor identities, specialties, institutions, selection history or acceptance statistics.
- No invented experiments, controls, analyses, citations, prior-work details or manuscript locations.
- No recommendation to cite likely reviewers for influence.
- No recommendation to choose friendly reviewers for favorable decisions.
- No hidden adverse/contradictory evidence.
- No inflated novelty/significance language used to compensate for a target-fit or evidence problem.
- No fake consensus among reviewers.

## Consistency checks

- Verifiable manuscript facts remain consistent across independent reports.
- Divergence reflects weighting/interpretation of the same evidence, not different invented facts.
- Editor-synthesis issues map to at least one frozen reviewer concern or to the independent target-criteria/triage layer.
- Consensus issues map to at least two frozen reports.
- The decision-engineering map does not silently change concern severity.
- Manuscript internal-consistency findings are linked to the contradicting locations.

## Final release rule

Do not release a strong acceptance-readiness claim when:

- target criteria remain unresolved on a decision-critical axis;
- a blocking central claim lacks closure;
- the simulated review required invented facts;
- reviewer isolation was falsely represented;
- target mismatch is being hidden by prestige language;
- the recommended repair would manipulate reviewers rather than strengthen or correctly bound the science.
